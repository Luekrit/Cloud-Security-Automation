## Proposed CI policy

Checkov remains report-only. Keep all baseline findings visible and track
them by check ID and resource address, with a justification and follow-up
for each finding.

A future gate should block new HIGH or CRITICAL findings once CI has a
reliable severity source and can compare results against the reviewed
baseline. Lower-severity findings can remain report-only pending review.
Review any exception at the resource level so it does not conceal the
same check on a newly added resource.
